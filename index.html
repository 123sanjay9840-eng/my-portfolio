// problem

Small growers sell through middlemen who take a large cut, and buyers rarely know where their produce came from or who grew it. Language gaps make direct trade harder still.

// solution

A local marketplace where sellers list produce directly and buyers chat with them in real time — with voice search and voice translation so language is never the barrier.

For sellers

  • List produce in minutes with photos and video
  • Set your own price — no middleman
  • Talk to buyers directly in your language

For buyers

  • Discover fresh produce from nearby growers
  • Search by voice, chat before you buy
  • Know exactly who grew your food
